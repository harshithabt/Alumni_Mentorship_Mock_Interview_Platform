# Alumni Mentorship & Mock Interview Platform

A Software Engineering Lab project for designing an Alumni Mentorship & Mock Interview Platform that connects students with alumni mentors for mentorship and mock interview sessions.

---

## Project Overview

The platform is designed to support the student–mentor workflow, from finding suitable mentors and checking availability to booking sessions, conducting mock interviews, and sharing structured feedback and interview experiences.

## Deliverable Folders

| Lab | Description |
|---|---|
| **Lab 1** | Requirements Engineering and System Requirements |
| **Lab 2** | Agile Backlog Creation and Sprint Simulation |
| **Lab 3** | Component Modelling and Architectural Pattern Selection |

## Key Features

- Domain-based mentor recommendation
- Mentor availability management
- Mentorship and mock interview booking
- Interview domain selection
- Structured interview scorecards
- Interview feedback and experience sharing
- Privacy and contact information protection
