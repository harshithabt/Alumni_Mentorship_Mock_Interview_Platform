## PROBLEM STATEMENT #06 

## Alumni Mentorship & Mock Interview Platform

---

This lab focuses on identifying and documenting the requirements for an Alumni Mentorship & Mock Interview Platform.

### What was covered

- Problem statement and system overview
- Stakeholder identification
- Functional Requirements
- Non-Functional Requirements
- Requirement prioritization
- Requirement analysis and documentation

### Key Features

- Domain-based mentor recommendation
- Mentor availability management
- Mentorship and mock interview booking
- Structured post-interview scorecard
- Interview experience sharing
- Privacy and contact information protection
